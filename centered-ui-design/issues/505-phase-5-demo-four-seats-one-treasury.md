## Current Behavior

No phase 5 demo.

## Intended Behavior

A four-seat hot-seat game, each seat preloaded with different priorities
(one all-in on glass shields, one on metal production, one on aether, one
balanced). Switching seats rotates the table. Shown: each tally as it
happens — every seat's ballot and weight, the resulting split — and a running
total of where every resource went, by category and by seat's quadrant.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. Preset ballots for the four seats in the demo's settings.
2. Tally log (data) and the tally display (view).
