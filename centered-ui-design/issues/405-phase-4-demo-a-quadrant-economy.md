## Intended Behavior

A scripted run of one quadrant: three factories with different locked
paths, units flowing to the front through the phase 3 console, engineers sent
mid-run to dismantle one and rebuild it facing a new direction. Shown live:
income and spending per resource as rolling readouts, units made per factory,
path lengths and walk times, and a chart (arc-shaped, in keeping with the
console) of the treasury over time.
