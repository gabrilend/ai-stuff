## The story so far

After the first picture, the owner asked for units with bodies, arms and
legs whose limbs deform rather than rotate, moved by updating a formula
rather than rebuilding shapes — so one figure mesh is built once and each
unit's walking phase, turned by the ground it covers, bends its limbs. And
a console shaped like half of the McDonald's M: the band the map's display,
the surface beneath it the dashboard, one arch per ally. Next the owner
asked for cuboids to carry, only a sliver of allies' arches with a chair to
turn, the band painted with the map from far beyond, and the real ceramic
engine instead of the Lua stand-in.
