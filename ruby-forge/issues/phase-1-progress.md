# Phase 1 Progress

## Goals
The smelter ladder: take in scrap of any mixed metals and separate it by
heating in rising steps, pouring off each material as it turns liquid, so
that every material comes out recovered and nothing is burned to slag by
heat meant for something denser. Each later model climbs higher and holds
temperature more precisely, toward a forge hot enough to fuse gemstone.
