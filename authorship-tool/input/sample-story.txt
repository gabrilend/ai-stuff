The Amulet of Echoes
====================

Chapter 1: Discovery

The old marketplace smelled of spices and rain-soaked wood. Elena wandered through
the narrow aisles, her fingers trailing across worn leather bindings and tarnished
bronze trinkets. She wasn't looking for anything in particular—just escaping the
suffocating walls of her apartment for a few hours.

That's when she saw it.

The amulet lay half-buried beneath a pile of moth-eaten scarves, its silver chain
tangled with cheap costume jewelry. But there was something about the pendant itself—
a smooth, dark stone that seemed to absorb the dim market light rather than reflect it.

"How much for this?" Elena held it up to the vendor, an elderly woman with kind eyes
and weathered hands.

The woman's expression shifted. For just a moment, something like fear flickered
across her face. "That one... you may have it. No charge."

Elena wanted to ask why, but the woman had already turned away, busying herself with
rearranging her wares. The amulet felt warm in Elena's palm, almost alive.

She should have left it there.


Chapter 2: Awakening

Three days passed before Elena actually wore the amulet. It sat on her dresser,
catching her eye each morning as she dressed for work. Finally, on a gray Tuesday
that promised nothing but meetings and mediocrity, she clasped it around her neck.

The world shifted.

Not dramatically—no flash of light, no sudden visions. Just a subtle change in the
quality of sound around her. Voices in the coffee shop seemed to carry weight, to
echo with meanings beyond their words. A businessman ordering his latte was also
negotiating a deal that would ruin three families. A student laughing with friends
was simultaneously grieving a loss she hadn't yet experienced.

Elena touched the amulet, and the echoes faded. Removed her hand, and they returned.

She spent the rest of the day experimenting, learning to control the gift—or curse—
that the stone provided. By evening, she understood: the amulet showed her the echoes
of people's true intentions, their hidden fears and secret hopes.

The question was: what would she do with this knowledge?


Chapter 3: Consequences

Marcus appeared at her door exactly one week after she'd found the amulet.

"You don't know me," he said, though something in his eyes suggested otherwise. "But
we need to talk about what you're wearing."

Elena's hand went instinctively to the pendant. Through it, she could hear Marcus's
echoes: desperation, duty, and something deeper—recognition.

"You've worn it before," she said.

He smiled, sad and knowing. "In another life, perhaps. May I come in?"

She should have said no. Every instinct screamed danger. But the amulet's echoes
showed her something else: that Marcus was her only chance at understanding what
she'd become tangled in.

"Five minutes," Elena said, stepping aside.

Marcus nodded and entered. As he passed, Elena caught another echo—one that chilled
her despite the warm evening. Time was running out. For both of them.

"The woman who gave you that amulet," Marcus began, settling into her worn armchair
as if he'd been there a thousand times before, "she was protecting you from those
who would have taken it by force. But her protection can only last so long."

"Who are you?" Elena demanded.

"Someone who wore that same amulet once. Someone who made the same choice you're
about to make: run, or fight."

The pendant grew warm against her skin.

Elena had a feeling her decision would change everything.
