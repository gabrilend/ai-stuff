# Open questions

Questions met while building, each waiting for the owner. A question here is
unanswered; when it is answered, the answer moves into the document or issue
it belongs to and the question is struck from this list.

1. **The book in the project folder.** chapters/ (the text of The Deed of
   Paksenarrion, 93 files), pictures/ (its cover and maps) and read-next (a
   reader script pointing at ~/documents) sit at the project root, untracked.
   The book is someone else's copyrighted text and the repository is pushed
   to GitHub. Should they be ignored by git, moved outside the project, or
   committed?
2. **Which button guards in first person?** The vision gives the mouse
   buttons to volleys and shields in third person and says little about the
   parry in first person. Built: right button held is the guard. Is that
   the intent, or should parrying be automatic, judged from where the sword
   sits on the U?
3. **Does the mouse move the sword or the eyes?** Built: the mouse moves the
   sword along its U; the eyes follow the lock; holding left alt frees the
   eyes. The vision says both "move the mouse to see" and that the U follows
   the nearest enemy.
4. **The 160 degree awareness.** Built as warm (allies) and cold (enemies)
   glows at the screen edges for soldiers beyond the 90 degree view, since
   the vision forbids interface. Is a glow too much interface?
5. **The campaign's rules on a loss.** Built: losing a battle gives the
   front region to the enemy and steps the front back toward home. The
   vision says only "decide which compass direction your contingent will
   push". Should the enemy also choose where battles happen?
6. **Critical hits are rare between minds** (about 2 in 100 hits, almost all
   from momentum; flanks almost never, because battalions meet face to
   face). Should minds step into their blows and seek flanks more, or should
   the momentum threshold drop?
7. **Screenshots of a covered window are black.** Pictures 011-016 in
   development-history/ were taken while another window covered the game
   or the window manager had resized it. Should demos run the game on a tag
   of its own (dwm), or render pictures off screen (into a canvas) so they
   never depend on the desktop?
8. **The feedback inbox** for the documentation website (issue 109): a file
   in the project, a local mail spool, or something else?
9. **Two players** (issue 702): versus (two commanders) or together (two
   soldiers on one side)?
