# Datapath: volleys and the shield wall

```
left click (third person) or the enemy commander's mind
   ──► if the cooldown allows: every standing soldier of the side who is
       not already in the melee throws one dart
       landing = its own position + its facing × range × battalion length
                 + a small scatter        (range is 3 without the trained arc)
   ──► darts fly the one arc for the flight time (drawn as a parabola)
   ──► landing: the nearest standing enemy within the hit radius takes it,
       unless turned aside: chance = shield wall bonus (large)
                                   + shield trait bonus + guarding bonus
       ──► otherwise damage × armor share, through the same effect step as
           a sword blow (011)

right click held (third person) ──► shields[team]: parry bonus against
   swords and (much more) against darts; the side walks slower

the commander mind (013): throws when the gap between the fronts, less
   the closing during the flight, puts its middle rank's darts on the
   enemy's middle rank; raises shields once per enemy volley seen, with a
   chance; lowers them when no enemy darts are in the air
```

The commander chooses when, not where; the ring drawn in third person shows
where the fixed arc will land.
