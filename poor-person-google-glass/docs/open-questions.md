# Open questions

1. **Safety interlock.** The light goes outward and the panel catches it. If
   the panel is moved aside, should releases stop (so nobody in front is hit
   by a laser), and how does the crown know the panel is in place without a
   camera — a switch in the arm's joint, a light sensor on the panel?
2. **Emitter colours.** One colour, or red, green and blue emitters for a
   colour picture?
3. **Slots per tab and tabs per pipe.** How fine is the picture meant to be —
   letters, icons, or images?
4. **What the store is for.** Physics says it holds light for microseconds,
   not seconds (docs/physics-check.md). Is its job pulse shaping (like a
   laser's Q-switch), or is there a longer storage the design needs?
5. **Hydraulic arm fluid.** Is the arm's fluid the same as the light pipe's
   filling, sharing one reservoir, or separate?
6. **The cuff.** Does it act as a stick, as a dial, or as its own kind of
   input?
