# Reference sizes

Typical published figures for a head-worn device the size of Google Glass,
and for the people and materials it meets. **Approximate**: each is a
starting point for a calculation, to be replaced by a measured or datasheet
value when a part is designed. The calculations in input/calculations/
name this file as the source of every number they take from it.

## The device and the frame it rides on
| What | Typical | Range | How sure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Glass, whole device | 40 g | 36–46 g by edition | published |
| spectacle lens width | 52 mm | 40–60 mm | frame catalogues |
| spectacle lens height | 32 mm | 25–42 mm | frame catalogues |
| bridge width | 18 mm | 14–24 mm | frame catalogues |
| temple arm length | 140 mm | 120–150 mm | frame catalogues |
| eye to lens (vertex distance) | 12 mm | 10–15 mm | optometry texts |

## People
