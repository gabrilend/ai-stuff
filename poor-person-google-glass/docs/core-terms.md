# Core terms

The words every other document uses. Each is a physical part or a piece of
data; where both, both are named.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **crown** | the whole device: a band that wraps around the head and rests like a crown, coloured like the wearer's hair (not made of hair). |
| **emitter** | a laser at the back of the crown, by the temple. There is more than one. |
| **light pipe** | the long passage from the emitters forward: solid glass, fibre-optic style, mirrored on its inside wall so light that strays stays inside. |
| **filling** | the seamless hydrogel or liquid the tabs move through. Its refractive index matches the glass so light crossing from glass to filling does not bend or reflect. |
| **tab** | a long thin strip that swings on an axis into or out of the light pipe. Several overlap along the pipe. |
| **slot** | one window along a tab's length, open or closed. A tab is a row of slots. |
| **mask** | the pattern of light that gets through all the overlapping tabs at once: light passes a point only where every tab covering that point has its slot open there. As data: a grid of on/off cells (booleans). |
| **store** | the rotating, internally mirrored cylinder section beyond the tabs. Light redirected by closed slots is kept circulating here rather than lost, so it does not disturb the flow down the centre. |
| **aperture** | the opening in the store. As the store rotates, the aperture lets a measured amount of light out at a time. |
| **release** | one opening of the aperture: the pulse of light that leaves the crown, shaped by the mask. Releases are intermittent so the crown does not collect heat. |
| **panel** | a small curved screen held in front of the face that catches the release. The light goes outward, toward the world, and the panel intercepts it. |
| **panel arm** | a hydraulic arm holding the panel, running along (or as an extension of) the side arm of the wearer's glasses: its pistons push it forward and bring it closer to the face, it turns at several joints, and the whole arm turns about its own axis to adjust the panel. Smooth and metallic, like a fine ink pen. The panel itself does not rotate; it curves. |
| **dial** | a rotating input on the crown's side arms: a forward one, a back one, and one up by the ear — three per side, six in all. |
| **A / B** | two physical buttons. No tap inputs anywhere: taps are triggered by accident. |
| **pocket stick** | a thumbstick carried in a pocket. |
| **cuff** | an input built into a sleeve or wrist cuff of outerwear, switchable on and off. |
| **heat fins** | heatsinks that lie layered on the hair and flare up and outward when hot, to give off heat the way sweat does. |
| **air pumps** | small pumps (no fans) that move air over the hot parts. |
