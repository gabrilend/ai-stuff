# Purpose

What the whole system is for, gathered from the vision (`notes/vision`, all
parts). The other documents describe the machinery; this one says what the
machinery is in service of.

## The aim

"A generalized, autonomous maneuvering agent that automates away capitalism
from us, because we just can't play nice."

Someone says "this is priced too high, could we make it cheaper?" or "it'd be
neat if this existed". The system takes that **wish** and builds what makes
it real: the software, the plans, the organisation, and the dealings with
other organisations. The people who wished it can then direct it together
("co-self-direct") instead of buying it from someone else.

The two first wishes, plastic wrap 1.5 times as wide and a self-feeding metal
workshop, are illustrations of the range, not targets.

## How work is done

- **Everyone works as they are bidden, human or AI.** The people who choose
  to work inside a venture are craftspeople and artisans who enjoy the work,
  or who feel called to it ("embidden"). The owner's own layout puts
  "bidden" directly above the "bidden" inside "embidden", marking the two as
  one thing: bidden from outside, and bidden from within.
- **Roles are tasks with a conversation.** Each role takes input and stays
  in back-and-forth with the person it serves. In this project, those roles
  are the **voices** (phase 7).
- **Real decisions about how to execute go through a test brigade** before
  they are made (`docs/open-questions.md`, 10: what the brigade is).
- **Organisation-to-organisation coordination can be done by AIs**, "so that
  everyone else can live as they please".

## Civic

"Civic is the noblesse oblige of the common man — it's what we owe to each
other." Not a legal form: a duty that every venture carries toward the
people around it.

## How distant-computer serves this

distant-computer is the workshop and its door. It gives an AI a machine of
its own to build on, and a way to hand its work back that a person can see
and answer (the gate). It keeps a record that cannot be quietly rewritten
(the ledger). And it keeps the rule that each side keeps its hands off the
other's parts when they are apart. The wish-to-venture agent is built on top
of this; where it lives is `docs/open-questions.md`, 14.

## The line that stays

Every action that reaches the world (money, contracts, sales, gifts, hiring)
remains an `outward` request that a person carries out
(`docs/architecture.md`, "Lines this design will not move"). "Automating
away capitalism" is pursued by making the *coordinating* work automatic:
finding, comparing, planning, drafting, building, and checking. Signing
in someone's name is not part of that. The record of who agreed to what is
part of what makes playing nice possible.
