## The shape of the phase


Phase 1 boots the smallest possible kernel on the Anbernic RG DS: power on into
our code, blink the LEDs as the earliest diagnostic, bring up USB device-mode
for a live debug serial stream, lay out memory, light both screens, and draw one
bright center pixel on each. The whole iteration loop runs over the USB cable
— no SD reader, no stock OS ever touching the laptop.


## Where it stands


The **foundation is solid and done** — toolchain, build system, boot vector,
exception handlers, memory layout, page allocator, and the LED diagnostic
channel all completed and several hardened by real hardware tests. The **hard
middle (USB + storage) is mostly written but stalled on hardware validation**,
and the **display half hasn't started**. Nothing is close to first-light on
screen yet.

## Open — and the one that's blocking everything


- **103g — watchdog silencing — URGENT.** The bootloader hands off the chip
  with its watchdog already ticking (~2.5s), so the kernel resets before
  finishing its earliest work. This is two MMIO writes at the top of
  `kernel_main` and it **blocks all further hardware bring-up**. This is the
  thing to do next.
