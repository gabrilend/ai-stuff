# 403 — the writer and the director

phase 4, the writers' room

**Depends on:** 402, 201

## Current behavior

Scenes and shots are written by hand.

## Intended behavior

The writer turns waypoints into `scenes.md` and cast/place declarations; the director turns scenes into `shot-list.lua`, marking gif shots and monologues. Role files in `assets/roles/`. setting-and-tone.md is part of the writer's instructions for the station film.

## Suggested implementation steps

1. Two role files.
2. Prompt the director with the shot-list vocabulary and an example.
