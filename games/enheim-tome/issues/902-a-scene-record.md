## Current behavior

Interactions can be typed. Nothing collects them into a thing that can be stored,
inspected, or handed to anybody.

## Intended behavior

One record, holding everything about one gathering where something happened.

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `hour` | when, on the global axis from [701](701-the-hour-is-global.md) |
| `place` | which block, with its natural character, its accumulated axes, and its status |
| `actors` | everyone present, each with their character and their open-or-closed |
| `interactions` | one entry per axis in play, typed by [901](901-an-interaction-is-one-axis-across-the-actors.md) |
| `spark` | whether two closed actors were intent on each other, and where the arc landed |
| `changed` | exactly which axes moved, on whom, from what to what |

### It is data, and that is the point of the whole phase

The scene is generated by arithmetic and is checkable with nothing on screen. The
words come later and come from this, never the other way round — see
[907](907-the-narrator-is-a-viewer.md).

Which means a scene record must be **complete enough to narrate from alone**. If a
narrator would need to go back and ask the simulation a question, the record is
missing a field.

### It is also the answer to "why is this place like this?"

A block's scenes, in order, are the sequence of gatherings that made it — each with
the arithmetic and, later, the words beside it. Nothing about a place's character
ever has to be inferred, because the steps that produced it are all still there.

## Suggested implementation steps

1. Define the record with those six fields and no others. A field that is only
   there for the narrator's convenience belongs in the narrator.
2. Snapshot the actors' characters **as they were entering the gathering**, not
   after. The record has to say what changed, which needs the before.
3. Make it serialisable on its own, so a scene can be written out, read back, and
   narrated without the simulation running.
4. Test the round trip: a written scene read back must narrate identically.
5. Test that every field a narrator needs is present, by narrating from a record
   with the simulation shut down.
