## The two systems that turned out to be one

A person's knowledge **is** the set of events they hold. A filter asking what they
know of hidden things here is a count of what they hold at this address, and a
filter answering nothing is them holding none.

**Nothing bridges knowledge and the map, because there is nothing to bridge.** The
hatching was already a picture of what somebody holds. That is why there is no
fog-of-war subsystem, no confidence channel and no discovery flags — all three
fall out of this arrangement rather than being built.

The structural consequence: holding is a relation between a person and an event,
stored apart from both. A `known` field on the event could only be known or not,
rather than known by particular people, and the whole per-person design would
collapse into one global state.

## The arithmetic that set the shipping line

| | How many | At thirty a day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| one per block | ~2,000 | one to two months |
| at least one per house | ~20,000–40,000 | two to four years |

Blocks before anyone plays; houses forever after. Two to four years of writing
before the first play is not a plan, it is an abandonment — including by the
author, who would not have played their own game for two years.

## The rule that is easiest to lose

> Just keep it from becoming a massive story. People live in cities, so what?

Every event local, ordinary, consequential only nearby. The variety comes from
tens of thousands of them, each written by looking at one real place on the
painting — **not from any one being remarkable**. Anything interesting to describe
to somebody who has not played is probably too big.

## The posture this phase sets for the whole project

**A place with nothing recorded is a place nobody has looked into yet.** Not an
error, not a to-do, not something a check may fail on. The city is always partly
unwritten and the program must be permanently comfortable with that — a tool that
nags about forty thousand missing items is a tool somebody turns off, and the
reporting that made the campaign legible goes with it.
