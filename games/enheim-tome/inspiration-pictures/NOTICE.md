# inspiration-pictures/ — reference material, and not ours

**Nothing in this directory belongs to this project.** These are other people's
artwork, gathered because they show the city this game is about better than any
description could. They are here to be looked at while designing.

## What is here

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `vision-map.png` | An oblique aerial painting of a walled city at a river's confluence. 6148×4092. The city the whole design is built around — the harbour, the ringed colonnade, the great willow, the three walled masses. |
| `vision-map-2.webp` | The same city seen flat and from directly above, 2518×2400, with its districts and gates already lettered — Northside, Duskside, Crosswater, Fineisle, Old City, Sunshore, Old Harbour, War Port, Mudside, Newtown, Ritterside. |

## What this notice does and does not do

It records that these are somebody else's work. **It does not grant anybody the
right to redistribute them**, and no notice written here could — that is the
author's to give.

## The consequence for the project, stated plainly

The design documents describe a game played over one painting, and during
development that painting is `vision-map.png`. **It is a stand-in.** It cannot
ship, and no amount of building around it changes that.

What this costs is smaller than it sounds, because almost nothing in the design
depends on *this* picture — it depends on there being one picture, in perspective,
of a city with streets in it. The fence network, the filters, the weave, the cage
and the tome all work over any such image. What would have to be redone is the
tracing, and only the tracing.

So the honest position: **develop against the stand-in, and treat every hour spent
tracing it as provisional** until either permission is obtained or original
artwork exists. That argues for building the tracing tool well before starting the
long tracing campaign, which is the order the roadmap already has.

Nothing in the program reads this directory. No build step, test, or documentation
page depends on it.
