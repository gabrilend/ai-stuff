hero-less-moba

Picture a lane-pusher, and subtract the heroes, the jungle and the items. What is left is a great many small bodies walking at each other and grinding to a halt in the middle -- which is not a game. So the things taken out are replaced by one shared chest of upgrades that three people argue over, and the argument is the game.
