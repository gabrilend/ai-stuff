a simple particle sim that we can use to generate .gif files.

usually black background, vibrant glowing particles (we can change that stuff)

okay true true okay so first we describe a motion, like "sweeping circle, slow
at first but then fast like a stroke. Two circles, both starting at 12o'clock
and sweeping to around 7. the particles are drawn at the tip of the hands, and
they are both oriented inward - the one on the left starts at 12 and goes to 7,
the one on the right is reversed such that it starts at 12 and sweeps around to
about 5, counterclockwise. After 5 for the left hand and 7 for the right hand,
fade in a line between 7 on the left clock and 5 on the right clock. Fill the
"triangle" between 7 on the left clock, 5 on the right clock, slowly."

that sort of thing. And it would render to like, 256x256 or larger .gif files,
not created through diffusion modeling, but rather through a particle sim, to
illustrate motion.
