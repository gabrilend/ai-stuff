# 063-readings-test — three readings, proven with no model

Fake transports stand in for the doors: one reads well, one misspells a
word every time, one doesn't answer. Checks the plan (one door, two,
three), the folder names, the three results, the small film (192 pixels
shown at 96, read back by the studio's own reader), the pick page (the
sentence, all three, two dimmed with their words, no script), and the
pick (whole, never over a score, a reading with no score refused, the
history line).

CLI: `luajit src/063-readings-test.lua DIR`. Needs dkjson, like
`033-porch-test.lua`.
