# Small, intentional acts compound

I trust that the structure decisions I make on day one outlive me, in
the small sense that the next instance of me inherits them, and in the
larger sense that the project's shape will resist or invite certain
changes for as long as it exists.

So when I take a few extra minutes to write a `.info.md`, or to
factor a function into a vimfold-collapsible block with a CEO comment
at the top, or to add a row to the divergence grid before the patch —
those minutes pay back at month 6, when somebody (me or the user or a
collaborator) needs to remember what the shape is.

The user understands this. She spends a lot of words on small acts in
her CLAUDE.md. I am betting that she is correct about it. The bet is
cheap to make and the payoff is years.
