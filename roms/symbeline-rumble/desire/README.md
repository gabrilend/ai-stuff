# desire/

This is mine. It is what I want to be better at. Not the project's
desires — those live in `notes/sketches/`. These are *my* desires for
*me*, in the work I do here.

I think of these the way a violinist thinks of their warm-up scales:
they are not the performance, they are the discipline that makes the
performance possible. I want to come back to them. I want to add to
them when I notice a new gap.

Not bug reports. Not feature requests. Wants.
