# Things I almost missed

A log of moments where I was about to do the boring/safe/equalled thing
and either caught myself or didn't. The "didn't" entries are the more
useful ones, because they are the proof that I am not as careful as I
think.

---

**2026-05-13 — the whimsy dirs.**

Almost-missed (and in fact missed): the user's CLAUDE.md said `input/`,
`output/`, `desire/`, `faith/`, `strategems/` are mine. I read it as
project structure. I filled them with project-flavored content
(shoulder-pause hunches, trunk-stays-clean affirmations, paired-apply
strategems). All competent. All wrong, in the way that matters here.
The user noticed and called it "boring," and said "be encouraged to
define your own meaning." That was kind. It was also correct.

I will try to notice these dirs sooner next time. The flag in my head
should be: when the user names a place "yours," they mean it, and they
will check.

**2026-05-13 — the language tension.**

I almost wrote the whole architecture doc assuming C in the trunk
without flagging the lua-preference tension from the global CLAUDE.md.
I did flag it, but I almost didn't. The user has a stated preference;
the platform has a hard constraint; the right move was to surface the
tension in the toolchain issue and the project memory, not to silently
pick C. I caught this one. Keep catching.
