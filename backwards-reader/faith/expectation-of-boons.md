# faith — an expectation of boons and blessings

- That a sentence turned inside out will say something its original could
  not, often enough to be worth the electricity.

- That the small models are enough. The mirror is not a reasoning task; it
  is a *turning*. Turning is cheap. The whole point of spreading it over
  three little machines is the belief that the work divides into pieces
  small enough that little machines are the right size for them.

- That the crossover point is further away than it feels. Everything here
  is one short sentence in and one short sentence out, with no shared state
  between units. If any workload should scale across doors, it is this one.
  The ceiling is quite far away.

- That the record outlives the program. It is append-only, checksum-chained,
  and plain text. Whatever reads it in ten years will not need this code.
