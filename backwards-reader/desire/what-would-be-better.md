# desire — what would be better

Written to as the work goes. Not issues; issues are blueprints for building.
This is the wanting that has not earned a blueprint yet.

- That the mirror of a sentence could be *checked* without a person reading
  it. The angle between the two embeddings is the first attempt at this. It
  is a crude instrument — it can tell "far" from "near" but not "far in the
  right direction" from "far because it became nonsense". A second axis is
  wanted. Perhaps: does the mirror, mirrored again, land near the original?
  A true reversal should be close to its own inverse. Nonsense will not
  come back.

- That the descent could find its own floor. Right now the depth is a
  number a person sets. It should be able to notice that a rung stopped
  paying — that mirroring clauses told us nothing that mirroring sentences
  had not already told us — and stop there, and say why.

- That the crossover point were a curve and not a number. It is measured
  per unit-size today. It really depends on unit size, door latency, model
  size, and how warm the KV cache is. A surface, not a point.

- That a reading of a reading were interesting. It should be: the record is
  text, so the reader can read its own output. Nobody has tried it yet.
  This is the [stack overflow] the vision names, approached from below
  instead of above.
