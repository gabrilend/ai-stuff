# What would be better

Notes on what this could become, kept apart from what it is.

---

**A turn you can play while walking.** The whole loop is text, so it could be
voice. Nothing in the design assumes a screen; the terminal is the first
surface, not the only possible one. The thing that would have to be right is
turn-taking, which is a harder problem than it sounds when the other party
sometimes needs thirty seconds to think.

**Standing orders.** From the house notes, years before this project:

> in dominions, it'd be nice if we could set up permanent loops of commander
> operations. Like, "move to this province, drop off units, move back, pick up
> units there, then move to this province, etc"
>
> think, factorio or spacechem. A more procedural generation of narrative
> events. something that is more aligned to the fantasy because it's a story
> that takes place across generations, as no religion's going to woo the world
> overnight.

This system is unusually well placed to grant that wish, because it already has
the two halves: a ledger that records intentions in its own words, and a hand
that translates them into the game's. A standing order is a ledger entry that
regenerates itself each turn against the current world. The game will never
support it; this could.

**The chronicle as a book.** Ten years of turns, three kinds of line, and the
game's own dated prose already attached to places. What comes out of that is a
history, and it would be worth reading. The reading half and the generating
half never touch, so a viewer could be written without disturbing anything.

**Reading mod files.** Six mods were loaded in the game read while building
this, and until `.dm` files are parsed, the narrator has to stay quiet about
anything it did not read as text from the save. That is the right restraint and
the wrong permanent state.

**A voice for the enemy.** Everything here is built on the turn file, which is
the fog of war made structural. Some day a game where two of these talk to each
other across that fog, each honestly limited to what its own nation knows.

**Somebody else's savegame.** Nothing in the reading is specific to this
collection, but everything has been tested against it. A second collection
would find the assumptions this one has hidden.

---

**A worry, written down so it is not lost.** The pleasure of Dominions is
partly in the interface — the density, the lists, the tooltips that go three
levels deep. A conversation is a narrower pipe. It should be honest about being
a different way to play rather than a replacement, and the measure of success
is that somebody who could not play at all now can, not that somebody who could
play prefers this.
