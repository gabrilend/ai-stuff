# Faith — expected boons

- That the two halves stay apart. If they do, either can be reborn in another
  language and the other will never notice.
- That the shared `.gitignore` remains one source of truth, and that reading it
  at runtime keeps this tool honest as that list grows.
- That policy descriptions, once written, make a drive feel less like a filing
  cabinet and more like a place you can take a walk.
- That the chronological thread is always there to return to when a walk gets
  lost — the anchor that never moves.
