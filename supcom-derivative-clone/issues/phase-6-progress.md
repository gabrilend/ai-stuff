# Phase 6 Progress — Watching It Happen

**The goal:** the real viewers. Everything up to here has been readable through a
terminal and a report; this is where it becomes a thing you look at and draw in.
Lenses, the always-open menu, drawing in the sand, the cloud in a corner, the
compass wheel, and the documentation's interactive pieces.

**Ends with:** a person placing a factory, drawing a pattern in the sand with a
mouse, pressing one of four buttons, launching a plane on the compass wheel, and
watching the cloud in a corner of the window.
