# 024-watching-it-happen

Phase 6: a lens push holds its anchor, the viewer reads snapshots only, a drawn
pattern becomes one command.

## What it claims

- Two hundred random pushes on random lenses never move the point under the
  cursor; screen-to-field and field-to-screen are inverses; an unknown layer is
  refused.
- Handing the viewer a snapshot does not touch the live world; the viewer holds
  what it was given and no reference to the world.
- Drawing a pattern issues exactly one placement command carrying every point,
  starting at the factory.
- The compass wheel issues exactly one launch command with a heading and the
  truck's id.

Pixels are looked at, not asserted; 602, 604, 606, 608, 609 and 610 are not
claimed here.

## Subjects it loads

`lenses` (603), `the-viewer` (601, 605, 607), `snapshot` (109), `the-world`
(104), `random-streams` (107).
