# spoken while building

The person's own words, kept verbatim, in the order they were said. Nothing
here is paraphrased and nothing here is tidied. When a phrase turns out to be
load-bearing, the document that leans on it points back to this file.

---

> hiiiiiii can you create this project with me? pink star squiggles!

*(the greeting that opened the project, 31 July 2026)*

**pink star squiggles** arrived before any of the four colour schemes in the
vision had been chosen, and before a single line of the client existed. It is
the first thing said about how the world should *look*, and it is three words
that each name a different layer of the same picture:

- **pink** — a hue, and one that appears in none of the four schemes written
  down in `notes/vision`. White-bright, black-bright, blue-muted, green-muted.
  Pink is the fifth thing, or it is the thing that crosses all four.
- **star** — a shape with points, radial, made of straight lines meeting at a
  centre. The vision asks for "lots of geometry and shapes"; a star is the
  geometry that reads as *light* rather than as *architecture*.
- **squiggles** — the opposite of geometry. A hand-drawn wandering line. The
  only word in the vision's neighbourhood that is not made of straight edges.

A world of squares and triangles, and the things alive in it are pink, are
stars, and squiggle. The terrain is rigid and the inhabitants are not. That
contrast is being treated as the visual thesis of the project until the person
says otherwise.
